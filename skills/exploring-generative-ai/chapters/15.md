## Core Idea

An LLM-assisted build benefits from a visible sequence of small decisions, artifacts, and corrections rather than one all-at-once request.

## Key Takeaways

1. Work through a concrete artifact incrementally.
2. Preserve intermediate checks that make regressions visible.
3. Use the model as a collaborator inside an engineering loop.
