# Chapter 5: Coding assistants do not replace pair programming

- **作者**：Birgitta Böckeler
- **日期**：2023-08-10
- **来源**：https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/05-not-your-pair-programmer.html

## Core Idea

Coding assistants can support tactical work but do not provide the communication, shared learning, focus, and team-practice benefits of pair programming.

## Key Takeaways

1. Do not equate suggestion generation with pairing.
2. Preserve human collaboration for knowledge exchange and feedback.
3. Use assistants as additions to a team practice, not replacements.
