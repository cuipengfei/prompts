# Chapter 13: The role of developer skills in agentic coding

- **作者**：Birgitta Böckeler
- **日期**：2025-03-25
- **来源**：https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/13-role-of-developer-skills.html

## Core Idea

Developer skills give agents reusable, explicit procedures and context; they make recurring work less dependent on an improvised prompt.

## Key Takeaways

1. Put repeatable judgment and workflow constraints in reusable skills.
2. Keep skills scoped to a recognizable situation.
3. Validate skill behavior with realistic pressure scenarios.
