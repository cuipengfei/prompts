# Chapter 8: How to tackle unreliability of coding assistants

- **作者**：Birgitta Böckeler
- **日期**：2023-11-29
- **来源**：https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/08-how-to-tackle-unreliability.html

## Core Idea

Reliability risk is lower when generated output has quick, trustworthy feedback loops and higher when verification is slow or unclear.

## Key Takeaways

1. Ask whether a fast feedback loop exists before relying on a suggestion.
2. Compilers, tests, linters, and review reduce uncertainty.
3. Increase caution when correctness is hard to observe.
