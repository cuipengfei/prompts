# Chapter 21: To vibe or not to vibe

- **作者**：Birgitta Böckeler
- **日期**：2025-09-23
- **来源**：https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/to-vibe-or-not-vibe.html

## Core Idea

Exploratory “vibe coding” is appropriate only when the probability of error, impact of failure, and detectability of problems make the result safe to discard or cheaply correct.

## Decision Rule

| Situation | Default |
|---|---|
| Low probability, low impact, high detectability | Exploration or vibe coding can be appropriate. |
| Mixed signals | Narrow the task, add checks, and review the result before reuse. |
| High probability, high impact, low detectability | Do not rely on vibe coding; require explicit design, verification, and human review. |

Probability depends on the tool, model, task fit, and available context. The required review depth rises with impact and falls only when detection is genuinely trustworthy.

## Key Takeaways

1. Use exploration where reversibility is high.
2. Raise rigor as consequences and maintenance needs rise.
3. Make the transition from prototype to production explicit.
