# Chapter 3: In-line assistance - when is it more useful?

- **作者**：Birgitta Böckeler
- **日期**：2023-08-01
- **来源**：https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/03-coding-assistance-when-useful.html

## Core Idea

In-line assistance is useful when it solves work faster at comparable quality after review, correction, and later maintenance costs are included.

## Key Takeaways

1. Measure usefulness by speed and comparable quality.
2. Task familiarity and prevalent technology affect suggestion quality.
3. Count review and rework cost, not only typing saved.
