# Chapter 9: Onboarding to a 'legacy' codebase with the help of AI

- **作者**：Birgitta Böckeler
- **日期**：2024-08-15
- **来源**：https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/09-ai-help-onboarding-codebase.html

## Core Idea

AI can help explore a large or legacy codebase, but it does not guarantee correct understanding or a complete solution.

## Key Takeaways

1. Use AI to form investigation hypotheses.
2. Verify claims against code and runtime evidence.
3. Treat onboarding support as navigation, not authority.
