# Chapter 19: I still care about the code

- **作者**：Birgitta Böckeler

## Core Idea

AI-assisted development keeps humans responsible for enduring code. Because generated output remains non-deterministic, risk assessment must precede the level of trust and review.

## Risk Assessment

Assess three dimensions together:

- **Probability**: how likely the AI setup, task, and available context are to produce an error.
- **Impact**: what happens if the error reaches users, operations, or on-call responsibility.
- **Detectability**: how likely existing checks and reviewers are to catch it before harm.

High impact plus low detectability requires strong human scrutiny even when the output appears plausible.

## Key Takeaways

1. Review code as an enduring product, not merely generated output.
2. Keep design and quality standards explicit when probability cannot be reduced to zero.
3. Do not trade understanding for short-term generation speed in high-impact, low-detectability work.
